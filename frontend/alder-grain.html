Discuss a commission

A little more
before we make it.

Questions about materials, commissions, and bringing a piece into your home.

How does a commission begin?

Send us a few measurements, photographs of the room, and an idea of what you need. We’ll discuss the possibilities and prepare an initial proposal.

Can I choose the timber?

Yes. We work primarily with oak, ash, and walnut. We’ll help you choose a suitable material and share samples before you commit.

How long will my piece take?

Lead times depend on the piece and our workshop schedule. We agree on an estimated delivery window with your quotation and keep you updated.

Can you work to my dimensions?

That’s the point of making to order. We’ll check proportions and practical limits, then agree on the final dimensions with you.

Do you arrange delivery?

Delivery and any installation are discussed separately and included in the final quote. Access requirements are checked before the piece leaves the workshop.

How should I care for the finish?

Each piece comes with care guidance for its specific finish. If it needs attention later, get in touch and we’ll advise on the right next step.

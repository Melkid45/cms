Before you pop in.
A few useful things.

A little about orders, availability, and enjoying your bake.

Can I reserve a loaf?

Send us a message with the loaf you have in mind and the day you’d like to collect. We’ll confirm what we can put aside before you make a special trip.

Do you take larger orders?

We’re happy to discuss pastry boxes and bread for gatherings. Please get in touch in advance so we can agree on the selection, quantity, and collection time.

What if something sells out?

We bake in small batches, so the selection changes through the day. Ask about a preorder if there’s something you particularly want to take home.

How do I check ingredients?

Please ask our team about the ingredients in any item before ordering. Recipes and seasonal specials can change, and we’ll help you find the information you need.

Why you’re not seeing results after a month of training

+

Progress isn’t always visible in the mirror first. Track the weights you lift, how you recover and how consistently you train. A month is a starting point, not a deadline.

Keep a simple training log. If your sessions feel easier or your repetitions increase, you are moving forward. Review the program with your coach before adding more work.

Training · 4 min read

Protein after workouts: myth or necessity?

+

Your overall daily nutrition matters more than a perfect post-workout window. A balanced meal after training can be a convenient way to support recovery.

Choose a routine you can repeat: regular meals, a source of protein and enough energy for your training. Ask your coach to adapt the plan to your schedule and preferences.

Training · 4 min read

How many rest days do you actually need?

+

Recovery belongs in the plan, not outside it. The right balance depends on the intensity of your sessions, your training experience and the rest of your week.

If performance is dropping or fatigue is persistent, discuss your workload with your coach. A walk, a lighter session or an extra rest day can be more useful than forcing another hard workout.

Training · 3 min read

5 habits that help your posture every day

+

Change positions regularly, take short walking breaks and build strength through a varied movement routine. There is no single perfect position to hold all day.

Start small: a break between meetings, a comfortable desk setup and exercises selected for your needs. If movement causes pain, seek individual advice from a qualified professional.

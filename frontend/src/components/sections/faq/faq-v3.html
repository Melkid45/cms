What to expect when we work together, from the first idea to the final detail.

Ask us a question

Where do we start?

We start with a conversation about your space, your priorities, and what you’d like to change. From there, we put together a clear scope and the next steps.

Can you work with our existing space?

Absolutely. We can rethink a layout, keep the pieces you love, and introduce changes that make your space work better without starting from scratch.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. We agree on a realistic schedule before we begin and keep you informed as the work progresses.

Can you work within a set budget?

Yes. We discuss your budget early and use it to guide the scope, materials, and priorities. Any changes are agreed with you before we move forward.

Do you help with delivery and installation?

We can support sourcing, coordinate with project partners, and oversee the final details. The level of support is defined in your project proposal.

What if we only need advice?

A consultation is a good place to start. We can review your space, explore possibilities, and leave you with practical recommendations to move forward.

Gather House

A shared workplace in London, designed for a team that needed room to work quietly and come together throughout the day.

Location
London, UK
Scope
Workplace interiors
Completed
2025

The brief

Make one open floor work for focused tasks, small meetings and team gatherings.

The approach

We put quiet rooms at the perimeter and gave the central space a shared table and flexible seating.

The result

Daylight reaches the centre of the floor, and the team can move between meetings and quiet work without leaving the space.

The practical things, explained simply.

How do I enquire about a room?

Send us your preferred dates, number of guests and room choice. We will reply with availability and the details of your stay.

Is breakfast included?

Our example room packages include a seasonal breakfast. Dietary requirements can be discussed with the team before arrival.

Can you help plan a longer stay?

Of course. Let us know what you have in mind and we can suggest a room and a pace that works for you.

What if my plans change?

Availability and cancellation terms are confirmed before you book. Please contact the team if you need to adjust your dates.

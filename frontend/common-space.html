Before you drop in.

Ask our hosts

Can I try the space for a day?

Yes. A day pass is a simple way to see how the space fits your working routine. Ask our hosts about availability before you come in.

Do I need to book a desk?

We recommend booking ahead, especially for a day pass. Members can check desk availability with the host team.

Are meeting rooms included?

Meeting rooms are booked separately. We’ll explain availability, rates, and any membership allowance before you make a booking.

Can I bring my team?

Absolutely. Tell us how many people you have and how you like to work. We’ll help you explore shared desks or a dedicated team room.
